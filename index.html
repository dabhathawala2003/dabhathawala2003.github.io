TTC Delay Analytics: a fully automated pipeline on live Toronto transit data

Where do Toronto’s subway, bus and streetcar delays really come from, and which stations get hit the hardest? I built a pipeline that answers that every single day without me touching it.

  • Pulls fresh delay data from the City of Toronto Open Data API with a Python Azure Function on a daily timer, saved to date-partitioned Blob Storage.
  • A scheduled Snowflake Task and stored procedure rebuild the warehouse daily: raw JSON, cleaned staging views, then a star schema (fact_delays, dim_delay_code, dim_date).
  • The dashboard shows buses carry about 80% of 2.57M delay minutes across 164K incidents, bus delays average 21 minutes against 2.7 for subway, diversions are the single biggest cause, and Kennedy Station is the worst hotspot.
  • Power BI dashboard with KPI cards, top delay causes, monthly trends, mode comparison and worst stations. Built in Import mode so the report keeps working even if the cloud trial ends.

Nytia Health: Complication Risk Prediction on Google Cloud

Capstone for Nytia Labs, an Ontario digital health company (Spring 2026). Which patients with chronic conditions are heading toward complications, so care teams can step in early?

  • Our nine-person team built an end-to-end pipeline on Google Cloud: BigQuery bronze, silver and gold layers, Cloud Run jobs for ingestion, features and training, and a live FastAPI prediction endpoint behind a dashboard.
  • Engineered compound features, like how many wellness areas are declining for each user. These correlated with risk at 0.69 while single areas were weak, showing risk comes from decline across many areas at once.
  • Validated the approach on the sponsor’s full 430 million records in BigQuery ML, where the XGBoost model reached 0.945 accuracy and 0.992 AUC.
  • Spotted that the full dataset was synthetically generated and reported it to the sponsor, so the results are read correctly: the pipeline is proven and ready for real clinical outcome labels.

Credit Risk Assessment & Loan Default Prediction

Which borrowers are likely to default, and what drives that risk? A scoring model plus a dashboard a credit team could use, built on 150,000+ customer records.

  • Cleaned the data, engineered 6 new risk features, and compared three models. XGBoost won with a 0.86 ROC-AUC, ahead of logistic regression and random forest.
  • Found late payment history is the strongest signal: borrowers with any late payment defaulted at 22.3%, against 2.7% for those without. The model’s risk tiers separate cleanly too: 93.7% of “very high risk” customers defaulted, against 2.9% of “very low risk”.
  • Loaded scores into a 5-table PostgreSQL schema, automated daily scoring with a Python pipeline, and published a Tableau dashboard of risk by segment.

Toronto Traffic Analytics

How has traffic across Toronto changed over three decades, and where is it heaviest? An automated pipeline over 32 years of City of Toronto counts (1993 to 2025).

  • Built a Python ETL that pulls from the City of Toronto Open Data API into a SQLite warehouse, then wrote 30+ SQL queries on 14,000+ traffic readings.
  • Showed traffic fell about 30% during 2020 to 2021 and has been recovering since, and identified Highway 401 as the busiest corridor at roughly 110,800 vehicles a day. Weekend counts averaged 8,817 a day against 6,656 on weekdays.
  • Published a Tableau dashboard with a 32-year trend, top 10 busiest locations and a geographic heat map.
